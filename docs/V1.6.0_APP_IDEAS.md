# MINT Access stem — App Ideas for v1.6.0 and Beyond

This document lists candidate apps for future development phases, compiled at
the start of the v1.6.0 development session (a small quantum-computing batch).

## Shipped in v1.6.0

- **qubit/** — A single qubit: the Bloch sphere, gate operations, Rabi
  oscillation, and measurement. The foundational piece of this batch —
  `bell/` and `grover/` build directly on the machinery introduced here.
  `gates` mode (default) applies a configurable gate sequence (H, T, H, S, X
  by default) to `|0>`, sonifying the Bloch vector's own `{x,y,z}` path
  continuously via lorenz/'s and henon/attractor's trajectory pipeline
  (reused directly, since the Bloch coordinates map onto the trajectory's
  spatial columns with no invented mapping needed), with a short accent tone
  marking the start of each new gate. `rabi` mode sonifies the exact
  closed-form Rabi solution, `P(1)(t) = sin(Omega t/2)^2`, as a continuous
  pitch glissando (compton/sweep's and relativity/'s phase-accumulation
  technique, reused directly) — the oscillation rate itself made audible.
  `measurement` mode simulates 2000 independent Born-rule measurements of a
  configurable superposition state and sonifies the running empirical
  frequency converging toward the true probability, reusing `rabi`'s own
  glissando technique rather than bayes/coin's spectral-density layering,
  since there is no evolving distribution shape here, only a converging
  estimate. Four correctness checks: gate unitarity (`U^dagger U = I` for 6
  fixed gates plus 3 rotation angles, exact, tight tolerance), Bloch length
  conservation (`|r|=1` after every gate in a 7-gate sequence, exact, tight
  tolerance), the Rabi formula against an independent `NDSolve` integration
  of the Schrodinger equation sharing no code with the closed form (exact,
  tight tolerance), and the Born rule via Monte Carlo (empirical frequency
  vs. `|alpha|^2` over 20000 trials, binomial-standard-error tolerance,
  matching scattering/'s check 4 convention). Realizes the `spin/` idea
  proposed in `docs/V1.5.0_APP_IDEAS.md`, generalized beyond a single fixed
  Larmor-precession picture into a full gates/Rabi/measurement single-qubit
  toolkit. See [`qubit/README.md`](../qubit/README.md) and
  [`qubit/AGENTS.md`](../qubit/AGENTS.md).

- **bell/** — Two entangled qubits: Bell correlations, the CHSH inequality,
  and repeated Bell-state measurement. Tells the EPR (1935) -> Bell's
  theorem (1964) -> Aspect's experiments (1980s) -> 2022 Nobel Prize story
  explicitly. `correlations` mode (default) sweeps the angle difference and
  sonifies the quantum correlation `E(a,b)=Cos[a-b]` (derived directly from
  the Bell state via explicit tensor-product operator arithmetic, verified
  a second independent way via diagonalisation + Born-rule probabilities)
  against a genuinely derived local hidden-variable prediction (not an
  arbitrary straight line — the actual triangular prediction of a shared-
  hidden-angle toy model, obtained via `Integrate`), binaural, reusing
  compton/discovery's and scattering/discovery's classical-vs-quantum
  idiom directly. `chsh` mode computes the actual CHSH value at angles
  found by global numerical optimization (`NMaximize`, confirmed
  symbolically to equal `2*Sqrt[2]` exactly — the Tsirelson bound, not
  assumed from memory, the single highest unverified-constant risk this
  app's build brief flagged explicitly) and sonifies it as a build-up-then-
  verdict gauge. `measurement` mode simulates thousands of paired
  measurements sampled from the true joint quantum distribution (not
  independent marginals), each side individually unpredictable 50/50
  (no-signalling, verified), converging to an unmistakable correlation.
  Four correctness checks: Bell state normalization (exact), the quantum
  correlation formula against an independent diagonalisation-based
  computation (exact), CHSH optimality (the derived angles give exactly
  `2*Sqrt[2]`, and a naive angle choice gives a strictly, meaningfully
  smaller `S`, proving the optimization mattered), and the classical
  `|S|<=2` bound proved by exhaustive enumeration of all 16 possible local
  deterministic strategies plus a complementary Monte Carlo sweep over
  random probabilistic mixtures of them. See [`bell/README.md`](../bell/README.md)
  and [`bell/AGENTS.md`](../bell/AGENTS.md).

- **grover/** — Grover's search algorithm: `O(Sqrt[N])` quantum search
  versus the `O(N)` any classical algorithm needs, a genuine proven
  speedup. The geometry is derived, not cited: starting from the uniform
  superposition, the oracle and diffusion operators are each reflections
  within a 2D subspace spanned by the marked state and the unmarked
  superposition, and two reflections compose to a rotation by `2*theta`
  (`Sin[theta]=1/Sqrt[N]`) — verified via explicit `NxN` matrix
  construction, not asserted from the standard textbook argument. `search`
  mode (default) sonifies `P(marked)=Sin[(2k+1)*theta]^2` as a discrete
  note per iteration, rising to a peak and audibly FALLING again past the
  optimal iteration count — a real, counter-intuitive fact ("more
  iterations is not always better") made directly audible, not just
  described. `compare` mode is a binaural race (the third member of this
  codebase's compton/discovery, scattering/discovery, bell/correlations
  classical-vs-quantum lineage): classical average-case linear search
  (`N/2` queries) on the left, Grover's optimal count on the right, both
  starting together — the literal duration difference IS the speedup.
  `geometry` mode sonifies the exact rotation directly as a continuous
  pan/pitch. Four correctness checks, all exact, including a genuinely
  discovered edge case documented rather than glossed over: naively
  searching "the argmax of P(marked)" over too wide a window can find a
  LATER peak that is numerically higher (at small N, where the discrete
  k-grid can happen to land closer to a later target angle) but is not
  the intended "optimal" answer, since more queries is never actually
  better even when a coincidence briefly suggests otherwise — fixed by
  sizing the search window from the oscillation's own period rather than
  an arbitrary large constant. Also discovered: N=2 is a fully degenerate
  case where P(marked)=0.5 for every k, and the common
  `k_opt~=(Pi/4)*Sqrt[N]` approximation, checked against the exact
  `Round[Pi/(4*theta)-1/2]` formula across eleven N values from 4 to
  1048576, is accurate to within +-1 throughout (worse in relative terms
  at small N, as expected). See [`grover/README.md`](../grover/README.md)
  and [`grover/AGENTS.md`](../grover/AGENTS.md).

This completes all three apps in the planned v1.6.0 quantum-computing
batch — `qubit/`, `bell/`, and `grover/` — start to finish in this
development phase.

- **quantum_statistics/** — Bose-Einstein, Fermi-Dirac, and Maxwell-Boltzmann
  occupation numbers — the quantum/thermo bridge, completing the connection
  `thermo/`'s classical Maxwell-Boltzmann speed distribution has always
  implied. The classical limit is verified as an EXACT algebraic identity,
  not merely an asymptote: with `x=(eps-mu)/kT`, `(n_BE-n_MB)/n_MB = n_BE`
  and `(n_FD-n_MB)/n_MB = -n_FD`, for every `x` — so the fractional
  deviation from classical literally equals the occupation number itself.
  `spectrum` mode (default) sonifies all three distributions simultaneously
  at a fixed T as three distinctly-panned, distinctly-registered voices
  (thermo/'s additive spectral-envelope technique, `SynthesizeAdditiveFrame`,
  reused three times over) — direct, simultaneous comparison rather than a
  sequential tour, since the mode's own question ("similar or different at
  this T?") can only be answered by hearing all three at once. `temperature`
  mode sweeps T at fixed energy and delivers a verified CORRECTION to the
  build brief's assumed direction: for this app's `mu=0` (photon/phonon-gas)
  convention, direct calculation shows the three distributions CONVERGE at
  low T and Bose-Einstein DIVERGES at high T — the reverse of the commonly
  quoted "cold=quantum" intuition, which specifically requires a large
  positive, T-independent chemical potential this convention doesn't have.
  `fermi_sea` mode sonifies Fermi-Dirac's textbook T->0 step function
  sharpening and blurring, using a genuine positive Fermi energy (not
  `mu=0`, which would only ever show half the step within the same
  non-negative energy domain Bose-Einstein needs elsewhere in this app).
  Four correctness checks, all exact: the classical-limit identity (tested
  at `(eps-mu)=10*kT`, relative error ~4.5e-5), the Fermi-Dirac bound
  (`n_FD<1`, direct algebraic consequence), the T->0 step's 10-90 transition
  width confirmed to scale EXACTLY linearly with `kT` (verified at three
  different T, not asserted at one), and Bose-Einstein's divergence as
  `eps->mu+` together with its `eps<=mu` domain guard both confirmed to
  actually engage. See
  [`quantum_statistics/README.md`](../quantum_statistics/README.md) and
  [`quantum_statistics/AGENTS.md`](../quantum_statistics/AGENTS.md).

## Apps discussed but not yet built

### Quantum mechanics

### Fluid dynamics

- **turbulence/** — the "harder" fluid dynamics follow-up to fluid/. 2D
  Navier-Stokes turbulence, energy cascade from large to small scales (Kolmogorov
  spectrum). Computationally heavier — may need vortex particle method at larger N
  or a pseudo-spectral approach. Flagged during fluid/ development as future work.

- **convection/** — Rayleigh-Bénard thermal convection cells. Hot plate below,
  cold plate above, fluid between. At critical Rayleigh number, steady rolls form;
  higher Ra gives chaotic convection. Connects to thermo/ (temperature-driven)
  and dynamical/ (bifurcation structure).

### Dynamical systems (extensions to dynamical/)

- **mandelbrot/** — Mandelbrot set and Julia sets. Iteration count as a 2D field,
  sonified via HilbertTraversalOrder from stem-core (same approach as images/).
  The boundary of the Mandelbrot set (where iteration count varies most) produces
  the most complex audio. Connects to images/ and dynamical/.

- **circle_map/** — quasiperiodicity and mode-locking (Arnold tongues). The
  standard map / circle map shows the transition from quasiperiodic to periodic
  orbits as a parameter sweeps through rational frequency ratios. Connects to
  resonance/ (same rational ratio structure) and dynamical/ (parameter sweeps).

- **lorenz_extensions/** — two additional strange attractors confirmed for the
  existing lorenz/ app: the Chen attractor (a=35, b=3, c=28 — similar butterfly
  shape to Lorenz but denser and more symmetric, discovered 1999) and the
  Halvorsen attractor (a=1.4 — cyclically symmetric three-wing shape, all three
  variables treated identically). Both were discussed at the start of the project
  alongside Lorenz and Rössler. Implement as new modes in lorenz/main.wl following
  the existing rossler mode pattern. Check lorenz/src/model.wl first to confirm
  current implementation state.
  Thomas attractor (cyclically symmetric), Halvorsen attractor,
  or Dadras attractor. The third attractor discussed at the start of the project
  alongside Lorenz and Rössler — confirm by reading lorenz/src/model.wl to see
  what modes are already implemented.

### Astrophysics and astronomy

- **stellar/** — stellar evolution on the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram. Stars move
  through luminosity-temperature space as they age: main sequence → red giant →
  white dwarf (or supernova). Map temperature → pitch, luminosity → volume, age
  → time. Follow several stars of different masses simultaneously as parallel
  voices. Connects to cosmology/ and hydrogen/ (stellar spectra are hydrogen
  absorption lines on a blackbody continuum).

- **pulsar/** — millisecond pulsar timing. Pulsars are the most precise natural
  clocks known. Their pulse arrival times can be measured to nanosecond precision.
  Sonify the pulse train directly (each pulse = one click), and the Doppler shift
  from orbital motion if the pulsar is in a binary. Connects to relativity/
  (pulsars are neutron stars — general relativistic effects are measurable) and
  resonance/ (pulsar timing arrays detect gravitational waves).

- **supernova/** — core collapse supernova neutrino burst and light curve.
  The neutrino burst (10 seconds, ~10^53 erg) precedes the optical brightening
  by hours. Sonify both channels: neutrino burst as a short intense burst,
  light curve as a slowly rising then slowly falling tone over weeks. The
  separation between the two channels encodes the neutrino mass upper limit.
  Connects to relativity/ and cosmology/.

- **nbody/** — N-body gravitational simulation (N=3 to 10). Full gravitational
  interaction, no restrictions. Chaotic for N≥3. Follow several particles
  simultaneously as parallel voices (same approach as magnetic/multi mode).
  Include some famous three-body choreographies (figure-eight orbit). Connects
  to lagrange/ (restricted three-body) and resonance/ (orbital mechanics).

### Celestial mechanics

- **tides/** — tidal forces and the Roche limit. A moon approaching a planet is
  pulled apart when tidal forces exceed self-gravity. Roche limit:
  d = 2.44 * R_planet * (ρ_planet/ρ_moon)^(1/3). Sonify: tidal force on the
  near vs far side of the moon → binaural stereo (differential force = the
  dangerous quantity). Connects to resonance/ (Saturn's rings are tidal debris
  inside the Roche limit) and lagrange/.

### Statistics and probability

- **markov/** — Markov chains and random processes. A particle hopping between
  states according to transition probabilities. Sonify: state → pitch; transition
  → note event; stationary distribution → the long-run pitch distribution
  (should converge to the eigenvalue of the transition matrix). Connects to
  montecarlo/ (Metropolis algorithm is a Markov chain) and bayes/.

### Electromagnetism and optics

- **maxwell/** — electromagnetic wave propagation. E and B fields oscillating
  in phase, propagating in the z-direction. Polarisation states (linear,
  circular, elliptical) → sonification. The wave itself IS an audio signal;
  map to audio with appropriate frequency scaling. Connects to signal/ (same
  wave equation) and waves/ (same mathematics, different physical medium).

- **diffraction/** — single and double slit diffraction and interference.
  Intensity pattern I(θ) as a function of angle. Sonify as a spectral sweep
  (same approach as resonance/kirkwood) — the diffraction minima appear as
  silences, maxima as tones. Double-slit interference fringes are directly
  audible as a regular pattern of loud/quiet. Connects to waves/ (same
  interference physics) and signal/ (Fourier relationship between aperture
  and diffraction pattern).

- **plasma/** — Debye shielding and plasma oscillations. Electrons in a plasma
  screen a test charge over the Debye length λ_D = sqrt(ε_0 kT / ne²). Plasma
  frequency ω_p = sqrt(ne²/ε_0 m_e) — electromagnetic waves below ω_p cannot
  propagate (ionosphere reflection). Sonify: plasma frequency → audio pitch;
  shielding cloud → spatial audio layer. Connects to magnetic/ (same charged
  particle physics) and thermo/ (statistical mechanics of charges).

### Special and general relativity

- **special_relativity/** — time dilation, length contraction, Doppler shift,
  twin paradox. A spaceship accelerating to relativistic speeds. Proper time
  vs coordinate time → two simultaneous audio tracks diverging. Relativistic
  Doppler shift of a signal → pitch change. Connects to relativity/ (same
  spacetime framework) and magnetic/ (relativistic cyclotron correction).
